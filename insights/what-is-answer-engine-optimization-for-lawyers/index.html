What is answer engine optimization for lawyers?

By Matt Hensler, Head of Strategy, Legal Marketing FirmReviewed November 27, 2026
Short answerAnswer engine optimization (AEO) for lawyers means structuring your content so search engines and AI assistants can lift a clear, accurate answer to a client’s question — and credit your firm. In practice: answer the question in the first two or three sentences, explain in more depth below, show who reviewed it and keep it current.

The AEO page pattern

  1. A heading that matches the question.
  2. A concise answer (40–60 words) immediately below.
  3. Deeper explanation, examples and exceptions.
  4. Named reviewer and review date.
  5. Related questions and internal links.

Where the questions come from

  • Intake calls and consultation notes
  • Search Console queries
  • People Also Ask results
  • Questions prospects type into AI tools

Example

Question: “How long do I have to report a work injury?” A strong page answers with the state-specific deadline first, then explains exceptions and what to do next.

Frequently asked questions

Does AEO need special markup?

Structured data helps machines understand content, but the clear answer on the page matters most.

Who should review answers?

A licensed attorney in the relevant jurisdiction.
